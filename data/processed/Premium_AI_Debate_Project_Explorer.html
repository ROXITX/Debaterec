Relation to Recommender Systems

NLP is responsible for converting free-text into structured preferences. The recommender then uses those preferences to retrieve and rank suitable items.

Agent Design

  1. Support Agent: argues FOR.
  2. Critic Agent: argues AGAINST.
  3. Judge Agent: compares both and ranks.
